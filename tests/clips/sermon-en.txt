Good morning, church. It is so good to see all of you here today.
Please open your Bibles with me to the Gospel of John, chapter three.
Jesus is talking with a man named Nicodemus, a teacher who came to him at night.
Nicodemus had questions, and Jesus did not send him away.
That is the first thing I want you to see this morning. You can bring your questions to God.
In verse sixteen, Jesus says that God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son.
Notice that the verse begins with love. It does not begin with our success or our failure.
Some of you came here tired this week. Some of you came here carrying grief.
Hear this clearly. The love of God is not a reward for good people. It is a gift for all people.
So when we leave this building today, let us carry that love into Chiang Mai.
Let us be patient with our families, honest at work, and kind to strangers.
Let us pray together before we sing.
