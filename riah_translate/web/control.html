Service

The translations can be changed while live.

Best: a cable from the sound desk. A microphone in the room works too, with more mistakes. A sound board with USB can send just the preacher's microphone: pick its channel.

Spoken translation

The translation read aloud into a headphone system: each language goes out of its own sound output, and the church's headphone transmitter carries it as that language's channel. Changes apply when you next press Start. Headphones only — never into speakers with the microphones open.

On the church screen

The bar and the column sit on top of the slideshow. Where there are no words, the slides show through.

Hide during songs: it keeps listening, the screen just goes dark.

Names and words to listen for

People, places and Bible books the preacher may say this week. The app spells them right more often.

Sermon notes

Add this week's notes (Word, PowerPoint, PDF or text). The app picks out the names and Bible passages to listen for. Nobody reads them, and they never leave this computer.
