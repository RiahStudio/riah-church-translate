# First Sunday with Riah Church Translate

English sermon → Thai on the projector (Chinese and English optional).

## This week, at home or the office

- [ ] **Check the church computer.** It can be a Mac or a Windows PC.
  - **Mac:** Apple menu → About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M…" → use **Riah-Church-Translate-mac**.
    "Processor: Intel…" → use **Riah-Church-Translate-mac-intel**. Needs macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later.
  - **Windows PC:** use **Riah-Church-Translate-windows**. Needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.
  - Either way, 8 GB of memory. On an older or slower computer the app picks a lighter speech
    model by itself and, if lines still come late, a quicker translation setting. Rehearsing on
    that computer shows whether it keeps up.
- [ ] **Install and open the app once on wifi.** First launch downloads the speech and
      translation models (about 2 GB). After that it needs no internet.
  - **Mac:** not signed by Apple yet. Right-click the app → Open, then System Settings → Privacy
    & Security → Open Anyway (macOS 11 and 12: System Preferences → Security & Privacy).
  - **Windows:** unzip the folder somewhere to keep it (Documents is fine), open it and
    double-click **Riah Church Translate**. Not signed yet, so Windows says "Windows protected your PC":
    press **More info → Run anyway**. Windows may ask about the network the first time: private
    networks only is right.
- [ ] **Allow the microphone.**
  - **Mac:** say yes when it asks (or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Riah
    Translate).
  - **Windows:** Settings → Privacy → Microphone: "Allow apps to access your microphone" and
    "Allow desktop apps to access your microphone" both on.
- [ ] **Rehearse with a recording** of this preacher if there is one: an old sermon video's
      sound works. It shows how his accent comes out before anyone sees it live.
- [ ] **Words box:** his name, the church's name, places and Bible books he's likely to say.
- [ ] **Sermon notes (optional):** press **Add notes** with this week's notes or slides. Nobody
      reads them; the app only picks out names and Bible passages to listen for.

## Midweek, in the church (the real dry run)

- [ ] **Sound from the sound board.** The app wants the preacher's microphone, not the band
      or the room. Three ways, best first:
  1. **The board's own USB, one channel per mic** (boards like the Behringer XR18 or X32,
     Soundcraft Ui24R, PreSonus StudioLive). One USB cable to the computer, pick the board under
     **Listen to**, then pick the preacher's mic under **Channel**.
  2. **A spare aux send** (or matrix out) with only the preacher's mic turned up in it, **pre-fader**
     so the sound person's main fader doesn't change it. Cable into a USB audio adapter (any
     $10–30 one with a line or mic input) into the computer.
  3. **The board's USB or record out with the main mix** (most small boards, like the Yamaha MG
     or Mackie ProFX). It works, but the band and announcements come through too, so lean on
     **Hide subtitles** during songs.
- [ ] **Level:** the bar on the operator page should move with his voice. The app evens the
      level out by itself; in tests it got the words right at 30 dB too quiet and when
      clipping, so close is fine. If the sound drops out it reconnects on its own.
- [ ] **Projector:** plug in and set the computer to *extend* the display, not mirror it (Mac:
      System Settings → Displays; Windows: press Windows key + P → Extend). Press **Open church
      screen**: it goes full screen on the projector.
- [ ] **Slides too?** Pick **Where the subtitles go → A bar along the bottom** (or the column on
      the right), start the slideshow and check the bar sits on top of it. Keynote: Settings →
      Slideshow → tick "Allow Mission Control, Dock and others to use screen" first.
- [ ] **Readability:** walk to the back row. Can you read the Thai at a glance? If not, raise
      **Text size** or drop to 2 lines. Untick **Show the spoken words too** for Thai only.
- [ ] Talk into the mic for five minutes, or play a sermon through the desk, and watch it run.

## Sunday morning

- [ ] Computer plugged into power. **Do Not Disturb / Focus** on (Windows: Focus assist), so no
      pop-ups land on the screen. The app keeps the screen from sleeping while it's listening.
- [ ] Close other apps (video calls, browsers with many tabs): the app needs the CPU.
- [ ] Open the app, check the languages, press **Start** before the service.
- [ ] **Songs:** press **Hide subtitles** (it keeps listening, the screen goes dark). Press
      **Show subtitles** when he starts preaching.
- [ ] Someone who reads Thai sits where they can see the screen and notes any word that is
      clearly wrong.

## Afterwards

- [ ] **Saved transcripts** (bottom of the operator page) has the whole service as text. Send
      it with the Thai reader's notes; wrong church words get fixed for next week.

## What to expect

- Words show as he says them; the Thai fills in during the sentence and settles a moment
  after it ends. A few seconds behind is normal, the same as the paid products.
- Machine translation: good enough to follow, not a trained interpreter. Idioms and jokes
  come out flat. Names in the Words box come out right more often.
- Silence, mumbling, or the preacher stepping off the mic are normal; the app carries on and
  needs nothing from you.
- If the screen looks stuck: check the level bar (is sound coming in?), then press **Restart**
  next to Stop. It starts the hearing afresh in about a second, and the church screen keeps
  its lines. A dropped feed reconnects by itself, and if the speech engine itself crashes or
  freezes the app swaps in a new one by itself (the screen says "reconnecting" and carries on
  within about half a minute). Only if Restart doesn't help, Stop and Start.
